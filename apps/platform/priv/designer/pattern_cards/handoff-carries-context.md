# Handoff carries context

**Pattern.** When work passes between a human and an agent, the receiver gets the goal, what was tried and the next step, without asking.

**Use when.** Agent to human escalations and human to agent delegations.

**Avoid when.** Never; a bare hand-off always costs a round trip.

**Evidence it worked.** No 'what is this about' replies after a hand-off.

# Stable layout under load

**Pattern.** Reserve space for content that loads late so nothing the person is aiming at moves.

**Use when.** Lists, feeds and toolbars that fill in asynchronously.

**Avoid when.** Never; there is no case where a jumping target helps.

**Evidence it worked.** No 'it keeps moving' or misclick complaints after load.

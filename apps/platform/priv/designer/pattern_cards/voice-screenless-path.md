# Voice screenless path

**Pattern.** Every voice flow can finish without a screen: options are spoken, few, and named by number or word.

**Use when.** Voice prototypes and phone or car use.

**Avoid when.** Content that is inherently visual (a chart); offer to send it instead.

**Evidence it worked.** Task success holds when the screenless persona runs the flow.

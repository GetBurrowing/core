# Voice confirm by echo

**Pattern.** Confirm a voice request by briefly echoing the key detail, not by asking 'are you sure'.

**Use when.** Spoken commands with one critical parameter (who, when, how much).

**Avoid when.** Trivial requests where an echo only slows the turn down.

**Evidence it worked.** Fewer corrected requests and fewer turns per completed task.

# Progressive disclosure

**Pattern.** Show the few controls most people need; reveal advanced options on demand, in place.

**Use when.** A surface has many options but most visits use two or three.

**Avoid when.** An expert tool where the advanced options are the daily ones.

**Evidence it worked.** Lower time to first action for new users without more 'how do I' for experts.

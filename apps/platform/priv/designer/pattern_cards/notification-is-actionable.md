# Notification is actionable

**Pattern.** A notification states what happened, why it matters to this person, and offers the one action it calls for.

**Use when.** Mentions, approvals, failures needing a human.

**Avoid when.** FYI events; batch them into a digest instead.

**Evidence it worked.** Higher act-on rate and fewer notifications muted.

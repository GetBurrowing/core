# Voice barge in

**Pattern.** Let people interrupt the agent mid-sentence; stop speaking at once and listen.

**Use when.** Any spoken response longer than one short sentence.

**Avoid when.** Safety notices that must be heard in full; keep those short instead.

**Evidence it worked.** Shorter calls and fewer people talking over the agent.

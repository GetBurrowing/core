# Batch low urgency

**Pattern.** Hold low-urgency updates and deliver them together at a predictable time.

**Use when.** Activity updates, FYIs, weekly summaries.

**Avoid when.** Anything time-critical or blocking another person.

**Evidence it worked.** Fewer interruptions with no rise in missed items.

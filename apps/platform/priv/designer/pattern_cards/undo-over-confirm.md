# Undo over confirm

**Pattern.** Let reversible actions happen at once and offer undo, instead of a confirmation dialog.

**Use when.** The action is reversible for a short window (archive, dismiss, move).

**Avoid when.** The action is destructive or external (send mail, delete forever, pay).

**Evidence it worked.** Fewer dialogs dismissed without reading; undo used and not complained about.

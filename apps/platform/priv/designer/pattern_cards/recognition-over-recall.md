# Recognition over recall

**Pattern.** Show recent, suggested or matching choices instead of asking people to remember names or ids.

**Use when.** Pickers for people, spaces, files or commands.

**Avoid when.** Secrets and credentials, which must not be suggested.

**Evidence it worked.** Fewer typos and fewer 'what was it called' questions.

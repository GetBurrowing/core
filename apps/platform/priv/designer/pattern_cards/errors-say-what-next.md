# Errors say what next

**Pattern.** An error message names what failed in plain words and the next step the person can take, with that step one tap away.

**Use when.** Any user-visible failure.

**Avoid when.** Internal diagnostics, which belong in logs not in the UI.

**Evidence it worked.** Fewer repeated attempts and fewer support questions after an error.
